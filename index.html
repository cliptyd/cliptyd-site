Cliptyd Clipboard history for iPhone. Paste what you copied earlier, not just the last thing. Coming soon to the App Store. What it does • Remembers your last 20 copies and lets you paste any of them. • Works as a full typing keyboard with your clipboard history one tap away. • Keeps everything on your iPhone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Set up in three steps 1 Open Settings > Cliptyd > Keyboards and switch on Cliptyd. 2 Switch on Allow Full Access. This lets the keyboard see what you copy and share its history with the app. Nothing leaves your iPhone. 3 In any app, hold the globe key and choose Cliptyd. Support Questions, problems or ideas?
